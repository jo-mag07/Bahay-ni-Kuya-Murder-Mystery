# Bahay ni Kuya — Murder Mystery

## Main Storyline

**Genre:** Detective / Murder Mystery / Puzzle  
**Language:** English  
**Player Perspective:** Second-person narration  
**Setting:** Bahay ni Kuya (BNK)

---

## 1. The Incident

`Dialogue 1:`
```
Something terrible has happened inside Bahay ni Kuya.

Kuya, the most important person in the house, has been murdered.

However, one important detail remains unresolved.

Kuya's body has not been found.

One of the suspects eventually revealed something that changed the direction of the investigation.

The body is hidden somewhere inside Bahay ni Kuya.

But finding it will not be easy.
```

`Dialogue 2:`
```
Several keys have been concealed throughout the house.

Each key provides access to another restricted location, but finding them will require solving puzzles.

The suspect refused to reveal anything more.

The remaining task is yours, Detective [Name].

Find Kuya's hidden body.
```

## 2. The Detective's Assignment

`Dialogue 3:`
```
You have been assigned to investigate the disappearance of Kuya's body.

Your objectives are:

1. Explore the accessible locations inside Bahay ni Kuya.
    
2. Discover the hidden keys and solve their puzzles.
    
3. Examine objects that may contain important information.
    
4. Remember numerical clues discovered throughout the house.
    
5. Gain access to every restricted room.
    
6. Discover the significance of the Notebook.
    
7. Locate the concealed area and find Kuya's body.
    

Your investigation begins in the Living Room.

Good luck, Detective [Name].
```

## 3. Beginning the Investigation — Living Room

The investigation begins in the Living Room.

It is the central area of Bahay ni Kuya, providing access to several parts of the house.

At first glance, everything appears relatively ordinary. Furniture remains in place, personal belongings are scattered throughout the room, and the house seems almost undisturbed.

However, the detective knows better than to trust appearances.

The Living Room contains several areas worth investigating:

- Sofa Area
    
- Dining Area
    
- Elevator Area
    

Each area contains objects that may reveal useful information.

Some objects provide ordinary observations, while others conceal important clues or items required to progress.

The first key must be found somewhere inside this room.

Without it, the investigation cannot proceed to the next restricted location.

## 4. The Confession Room

After obtaining the appropriate key, the detective gains access to the Confession Room.

Unlike the Living Room, this area feels considerably more personal.

It is a place where housemates would normally speak privately, express their thoughts, and discuss matters they might not openly share elsewhere.

The room contains several objects worth examining.

Among them is a wall clock.

Its displayed time may seem insignificant at first, but the detective should pay attention to the details.

The room also contains another hidden key, protected by a separate puzzle.

Finding it will allow the detective to continue the investigation.

## 5. The Garden

```
With the appropriate key, the detective gains access to the Garden.

The atmosphere outside is noticeably different from the enclosed rooms.
```

The Garden contains several features that deserve investigation:

```
- Flower Bed
    
- Garden Bench
    
- Bird Bath
    
- Watering Can
    
- Garden Lamp
    
- Garden Statue
```

```
Each object has its own purpose and may reveal something worth examining.

The Flower Bed contains an important numerical clue, although its significance is not immediately explained.

The Garden Statue also conceals a puzzle that must be solved to obtain the next key.

Despite being thoroughly examined, the Garden appears to offer no immediate explanation for Kuya's disappearance.

The detective continues searching for the remaining keys.
```

## 6. The Girls Bedroom

The next restricted location is the Girls Bedroom.

Upon entering, the detective finds an assortment of personal belongings and everyday objects.

The room appears ordinary, but several details deserve closer attention.

Among the objects is a wardrobe.

Its contents provide another numerical clue that the detective must remember.

Other objects may reveal additional information about the people who stayed in the room.

Some discoveries may appear insignificant until they are considered alongside evidence from other locations.

The detective must continue the investigation and locate the next key.

## 7. The Boys Bedroom

The final restricted location is the Boys Bedroom.

By this point, the detective has successfully gained access to every major location inside Bahay ni Kuya.

However, the investigation is far from over.

While examining the room, the detective discovers a Notebook.

Unlike the other objects found throughout the house, the Notebook appears to contain information deliberately concealed from anyone who might discover it.

Its contents are protected by another riddle.

The detective must solve it before gaining access.

## 8. The Notebook's Revelation

Upon successfully opening the Notebook, the detective discovers a message.

The message indicates that the investigation is incomplete.

It instructs the detective to revisit the previously explored locations because one of them contains something that was not immediately visible during the initial investigation.

The message does not directly reveal where Kuya's body is hidden.

Instead, it provides enough information to suggest that the detective has overlooked something important.

The investigation returns to the Garden.

## 9. The Hidden Area

Upon revisiting the Garden, the detective notices something unusual.

A previously unremarkable section of the area can now be investigated.

The Artificial Grass Floor.

Something appears to be protruding beneath it.

Upon examining the area, the detective discovers a concealed door.

However, the door is secured by a numerical password.

A riddle provides instructions on how to construct the password using four numerical clues discovered earlier in the investigation.

The detective must remember the numbers and arrange them in the correct sequence.

The four clues are:

- Confession Room — Wall Clock
    
- Living Room — Floor Indicator
    
- Girls Bedroom — Wardrobe
    
- Garden — Flower Bed
    

The correct sequence is determined by the riddle rather than the order in which the rooms were explored.

## 10. The Final Discovery

After entering the correct password, the concealed door finally unlocks.

The detective slowly enters the hidden area.

Inside, the truth that had been concealed throughout the investigation is finally revealed.

Kuya's body is discovered.

The search that began with an unexplained disappearance has finally reached its most disturbing conclusion.

The detective has successfully uncovered the location where the victim was hidden.

**END OF THE MAIN INVESTIGATION.**

---

## 11. Narrative Design Notes

- The player's chosen detective name must be incorporated into appropriate dialogue and narrative messages.
    
- The game must never reveal the Garden's hidden area during the initial investigation.
    
- Numerical clues should be presented naturally through object descriptions.
    
- The significance of the four numbers should not be explained until the final password riddle.
    
- The Notebook must be inaccessible until its riddle is solved.
    
- The Artificial Grass Floor must only become an available investigation option after the Notebook is accessed.
    
- The final password must be constructed using the four previously discovered numbers.
    
- The body must only be revealed after the concealed door is successfully unlocked.
    
- All keys, riddles, and their corresponding rewards must be predetermined.
