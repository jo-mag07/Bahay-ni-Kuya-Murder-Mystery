**Location ID:** RM-002
**Status:** Proposed Design
**Role:** Second Investigation Location

## 1.1 Overview

```
The Confession Room is a private space where housemates traditionally share their thoughts and experiences.

As you enter, you notice a quiet and enclosed room. A chair sits in front of recording equipment, while soundproof panels cover parts of the walls.

Everything appears arranged for private conversations, with several objects waiting to be examined.

You may investigate the available objects or return to the Living Room.
```

<>

### 1.2 Room Entry Message

```
The Confession Room door opens.

You step inside and take a moment to examine your surroundings.

The room is quiet, with recording equipment and furniture arranged throughout the space.

Perhaps something here will help you continue the investigation.
```

## 3. Object Purposes

### Broken Wall Clock
```
An old wall clock hangs above the room. Its hands have stopped moving, frozen at exactly 9:00 PM.

The clock no longer works, but the displayed time remains visible.
```

### Confession Chair
```
A chair placed in front of the recording equipment, where housemates would sit during their confession sessions.
```

### Microphone
```
A microphone used to capture the words spoken during confession sessions. It appears to be in working condition.
```

### Camera
```
A camera positioned to record activities inside the room. Its lens faces the confession area.
```

### Side Cabinet
```
A small cabinet stands against the wall. Its contents appear ordinary at first, but something about it suggests there may be more hidden inside.
```
### Wall Panels
```
Soundproof panels cover the walls, helping keep conversations inside the room private.
```

## 6. Navigation

```
The player can investigate all six objects.

The player may return to the Living Room after completing an investigation.

The Garden remains inaccessible until the Garden Key is obtained.
```

## 7. Gameplay Rules

```
1. Ordinary objects display descriptions and a Return option.
2. Puzzle objects provide additional investigation options.
3. Incorrect answers do not award the key.
4. The player may retry riddles without penalties.
5. Correct answers award the predetermined key.
6. Collected keys cannot be obtained repeatedly.
   
   ## Repeat Investigation Behavior

When the player investigates the Side Cabinet after obtaining the Garden Key:

The concealed compartment is already open. The Garden Key has already been collected.

The riddle must not appear again, and the key must not be awarded again.
```
