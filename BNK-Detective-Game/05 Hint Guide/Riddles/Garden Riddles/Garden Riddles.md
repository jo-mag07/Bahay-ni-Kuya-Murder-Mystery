## RD-003 — The Silent Figure

- **Riddle ID:** RD-003
- **Location:** Garden
- **Object:** Garden Statue
- **Answer:** STATUE
- **Reward:** Girls Bedroom Key (KY-003)
- **Status:** Confirmed

### Riddle Text

```
"I have no voice, yet I tell a tale.
I stand in place through sun and gale.
I never walk, yet I may be moved.
What am I?"
```

Answer: `STATUE`

### Correct Answer Response

```
"The statue makes a faint clicking sound.

A small compartment opens beneath its base.

Inside, you discover a key."

**Reward obtained:** Girls Bedroom Key (KY-003).
```

### Incorrect Answer Response

"Nothing happens. The statue remains still."

Options:
1. Try Again
2. Return

### Retry Rules

```
- Answers are case-insensitive.
- Leading and trailing spaces should be ignored.
- Unlimited attempts are allowed.
- Incorrect answers have no penalty.
- The player can return without losing progress.
- The key can only be collected once.
```

### Optional Hints

```
1. It does not move on its own.
2. It is an object commonly found in gardens.
3. It can be made to resemble a person, animal, or other figure.
```

## RD-006 — The Final Discovery

- **Riddle ID:** RD-006
- **Location:** Garden — Hidden Chamber
- **Object:** Final Discovery
- **Answer:** KUYA
- **Reward:** Game Completion
- **Status:** Confirmed

### Riddle Text

"I was hidden from every eye.

Beneath the place where flowers lie.

No footsteps lead to where I stay.

Yet here I rest, concealed away.

What is it that you have come to find?"

### Correct Answer

**KUYA**

### Correct Answer Response

"Your eyes widen.

The truth of the suspect's final clue becomes clear.

Hidden beneath the Garden, you finally discover Kuya.

Your investigation is complete."

### Incorrect Answer Response

"The answer does not seem right.

You look around the hidden chamber, trying to understand the clue."

Options:
1. Try Again
2. Return

### Retry Rules

- Answers are case-insensitive.
- Leading and trailing spaces should be ignored.
- Unlimited attempts are allowed.
- Incorrect answers have no penalty.

### Completion Effect

After the player correctly answers:

1. Kuya is discovered.
2. The investigation is marked complete.
3. The game ends immediately.

### Important Rules

- The correct answer is KUYA, not KUYA'S BODY.
- The riddle must only appear after the concealed door is opened.
- The game does not investigate the murderer's identity, motive, or guilt.
