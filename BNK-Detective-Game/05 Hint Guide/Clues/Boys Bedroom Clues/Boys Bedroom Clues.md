## IT-001 — Notebook

- **Item ID:** IT-001
    
- **Item Name:** Notebook
    
- **Location:** Boys Bedroom
    
- **Object:** Study Desk
    
- **Item Type:** Special Progression Item
    
- **Puzzle:** RD-005
    
- **Answer:** NOTEBOOK
    
- **Reward:** Access to Notebook contents
    
- **Numerical Clue:** None
    
- **Status:** `Confirmed`
    

### Player Discovery

When the player investigates the Study Desk, the Notebook becomes available for examination.

The Notebook is protected by a riddle titled **The Keeper of Thoughts**.

The player must provide the correct answer, `NOTEBOOK`, to access its contents.

### Notebook Contents

Upon successfully solving the riddle, display:

```
You have access to every room in this house.

But finding the keys was only the beginning.

Return to each room. Look closely. There is evidence you have yet to understand.

Do not overlook anything.
```

### Clue Purpose

The Notebook provides information that advances the investigation to its final phase.

Its contents instruct the detective to revisit previously explored locations and review existing evidence.

The Notebook does not directly reveal the location of Kuya's body.

### Conditional Effect

After the Notebook has been successfully accessed:

1. Activate the Final Investigation Phase.
    
2. Allow the player to revisit previously explored locations.
    
3. Make the Artificial Grass Floor available as an additional investigation option in the Garden.
    
4. Allow the player to discover the concealed door beneath the artificial grass.
    

The Artificial Grass Floor must remain unavailable before the Notebook is accessed.

### Repeat Investigation Behavior

- The Notebook riddle must not appear again after successful access.
    
- The Notebook contents must not be displayed repeatedly.
    
- The Notebook must remain recorded in the player's inventory.
    
- The Final Investigation Phase must not be activated repeatedly.
