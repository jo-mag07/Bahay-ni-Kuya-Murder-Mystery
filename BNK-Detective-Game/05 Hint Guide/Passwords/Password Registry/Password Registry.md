## PW-001 — Garden Concealed Door

- **Password ID:** PW-001
- **Password:** 9364
- **Location:** Garden — Concealed Door
- **Type:** Final Numerical Password
- **Status:** `Confirmed`

### Password Sequence

| Order | Location | Object | Numerical Value |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Confession Room | Wall Clock | 9 |
| 2 | Living Room | Floor Indicator | 3 |
| 3 | Girls Bedroom | Wardrobe | 6 |
| 4 | Garden | Flower Bed | 4 |

### Password Explanation

The player must combine the four numerical clues in the exact order given by the concealed door inscription.

**9 + 3 + 6 + 4 =** `9364`

The password is entered into the keypad beneath the Garden.

### Validation Rules

- Correct password: 9364.
- Incorrect password: Door remains locked.
- Unlimited attempts.
- No penalty for incorrect attempts.

### Unlock Effect

Entering 9364 correctly opens the concealed door and allows the player to enter the hidden chamber.
